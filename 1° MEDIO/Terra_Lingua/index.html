❔ How to play

  • WASD move · Mouse look · Space jump · Shift run · E talk
  • J missions · B shop · P party · T chat · M map · V camera
  • Complete the 8 missions of each unit to open the next land.
  • Wrong answer? Try again. On the third try you get feedback.
  • Click attack · Right click or R power (fire, ice, paint…). On the phone: ✖ attack, ✨ power.
  • Each land has its own creatures and a 👑 boss. Defeat them for coins. Purple chests 💜 hide on towers, ships and ruins (◆ on the map).
  • Each land sells new weapons and powers in the shop 🎒.
  • Creative: left click removes, right click places, F flies.

Characters, creatures, sounds and music: Kenney (CC0), CoMiGo (CC0).
